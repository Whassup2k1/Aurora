Aurora — Public Roadmap
Your media. Your server. Your world.

Aurora is evolving into a unified, self-hosted entertainment ecosystem: Aurora Server, Aurora Desktop, Aurora Experience, Aurora Android, and the Aurora Cinema Engine.

This page is a public product roadmap, not the internal engineering backlog. It describes direction and maturity without exposing private implementation details, internal builds, credentials, infrastructure, or target dates.
Development status: Aurora is in private development and validation. There are no public builds or downloads at this time. A component working in a private environment is not necessarily ready for public distribution.

How to read this roadmap
Label	Meaning
Implemented internally	Core functionality exists and is used or evaluated in private environments.
In validation	Functionality is being tested, stabilized, or integrated across devices and environments.
In development	Work is actively underway, but the scope or behaviour may still change.
Planned	A product direction or concept, not a confirmed release commitment.


These labels apply to areas of the product, not to every feature on every platform. Exact implementation, compatibility, and availability can vary.
The Aurora ecosystem
Aurora Server — Implemented internally / In validation
The self-hosted foundation behind Aurora's libraries, metadata, users, streaming, administration, and media access.
- Linux: private server implementation and packaging work; reliability and installation validation remain priorities.
- macOS: private server implementation for Apple silicon; packaging, updates, and recovery continue to be validated.
- Windows: planned / upcoming development, not currently presented as a supported public server platform.
Next priorities: dependable clean installation, safe updates and recovery, storage and library handling, diagnostics, security hardening, and cross-platform consistency.
Aurora Desktop — Implemented internally / In validation
A full-featured interface for organizing, discovering, managing, and enjoying personal media.

Next priorities: consistent navigation and playback, polish, accessibility, performance, and reliable interaction with Aurora Server.
Aurora Experience — Implemented internally / In validation
An immersive fullscreen experience for televisions and desktop displays, designed with remote, keyboard, and controller navigation in mind.

Next priorities: stable focus/navigation behavior, TV guide responsiveness, consistent video playback, and a smooth transition between Aurora's experiences.
Aurora Android — Implemented internally / In validation
A dedicated Android client is functional in private testing. Compatibility, installation, signing, updates, and real-device playback require further validation before public availability.

Next priorities: device compatibility, remote-friendly interaction where applicable, playback robustness, and reproducible distribution.
Aurora Cinema Engine — In development / In validation
Aurora's evolving video playback and processing work, designed to support a coherent viewing experience across compatible clients and devices.

Next priorities: dependable start-up, direct playback where supported, streaming/transcoding fallback where necessary, seeking, subtitles, audio compatibility, and performance testing.
Playback capabilities depend on the media format, codecs, device, network, and server hardware. No universal playback or transcoding performance guarantee is implied.

Media experiences
Music — Implemented internally / Continuing improvement
- Personal music libraries, artists, albums, playlists, favorites, queue, and search.
- Artwork, metadata, and high-quality playback workflows.
- Fullscreen listening and a rich discovery-oriented interface.
- Continued refinement of playback controls, metadata quality, and personalization.
Movies & Series — Implemented internally / Continuing improvement
- Personal movie and television-series libraries with artwork and contextual metadata.
- Dedicated discovery and detail-page experiences.
- Playback progress and ongoing improvements to browsing and viewing flows.
- Continued work on collections, studios, people pages, artwork, and cross-media discovery.
Live TV & IPTV — Implemented internally / In validation
- Channel browsing and TV-focused playback experiences.
- Guide and navigation improvements, including responsiveness and reliability.
- Ongoing work on compatibility, playback behavior, and source management.
Aurora is intended for media and sources users are authorized to access. Aurora does not provide television channels or third-party media subscriptions.
Karaoke & Lyrics — Implemented internally / Continuing improvement
- Local lyric workflows and synchronized-lyrics foundations.
- Karaoke-oriented views integrated with the music library.
- Ongoing work on synchronization, editing, and more complete party-oriented features.
Guitar Tabs & Practice — Implemented internally / Continuing improvement
- A guitar-learning and practice environment built around tablature and music playback workflows.
- Continued improvement of practice controls, compatibility, and usability.
News & Discovery — Implemented internally / Continuing improvement
- Integrated music news and related discovery experiences.
- Continued work on content presentation, attribution, metadata, and language handling.
Languages & accessibility
French and English are supported in the private Aurora interface. Broader localization coverage and consistency continue to be tested across the ecosystem.

Additional languages are planned, but none are announced as available until translation and interface validation are complete.

Aurora will also continue improving keyboard/controller navigation, focus visibility, responsiveness, and readability across screen sizes.
Before public Early Access
The next milestone is a dependable private-to-public beta foundation, not the completion of every long-term idea.

Priorities include:
1. Installation, updates, backup, recovery, and clean-system validation.
2. Server security and protection of private implementation details.
3. Reliable playback and compatibility across Aurora Desktop, Experience, and Android.
4. Cinema Engine playback testing across realistic files, devices, and network conditions.
5. Television guide, IPTV, and navigation stability.
6. Consistent language support and interface quality.
7. Documentation, issue reporting, privacy information, and a clear Early Access process.
No public release date has been committed. Public availability will be announced here when there is a build suitable for external testers.

Longer-term directions — Planned
These are ideas for exploration after core reliability and distribution are established. They are not promises of availability or delivery order.
- More device platforms: explore wider television, mobile, and desktop coverage, including Apple ecosystems where technically and operationally feasible.
- Aurora Sessions: optional shared viewing, listening, or synchronized multi-device experiences.
- Aurora Chat: optional communication features connected to shared media experiences.
- A richer unified Home: bring media types together while preserving dedicated experiences for music, cinema, television, and more.
- Broader localization: expand beyond French and English after translation quality and interface coverage can be verified.
- Library discovery: improved collections, studios, recommendations, and personalized navigation.
What will remain important
Local-first & user control
Aurora is built for personal libraries hosted on infrastructure controlled by their owners. Optional external services may be used for particular features; exact connectivity and privacy behavior will be described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
Reliability before expansion
Playback quality, installation safety, backups, data integrity, clear troubleshooting, and good navigation matter more than adding features simply to make the roadmap longer.
Responsible availability
Aurora is proprietary software. Its public GitHub repository is a project information and community space; it is not a public source-code distribution. Public documents do not grant access to private code or imply downloadable releases are available.
Community participation
Interested in testing Aurora or sharing feedback? Read the [Early Access information](EARLY_ACCESS.md) and use the project's GitHub Discussions where appropriate.

Feedback helps improve priorities, but this roadmap remains subject to technical validation, product direction, resources, and safety requirements.
