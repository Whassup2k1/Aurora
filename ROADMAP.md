# Aurora Roadmap

Aurora is being built as a personal self-streaming platform designed to bring your media, entertainment and connected experiences together under one unified environment.

This roadmap reflects the current direction of Aurora.

Priorities may evolve as the platform matures, testing expands and community feedback helps identify the areas that matter most.

The roadmap represents direction rather than guaranteed release dates.

---

## 🌌 Current Foundation

Aurora already has a substantial native foundation powering the platform.

Current systems include:

- Aurora Server
- Native media architecture
- Music catalog and library scanning
- High-quality audio streaming and seeking
- Albums, artists, playlists and favorites
- Global search
- Artwork and metadata management
- Multi-user foundation
- Administration environment
- Integrations and metadata services
- Backup and update infrastructure
- Local HTTPS and network discovery
- Linux installation and packaging
- Karaoke foundation
- Guitar Tabs and practice environment
- Music News
- Movies and Series foundation
- TV foundation

---

## 🚀 First Public Beta

The immediate priority is preparing Aurora for its first public beta.

Before broader distribution, the focus is on:

- Installation reliability
- Upgrade reliability
- Backup and recovery validation
- Server stability
- Library scanning reliability
- Music playback stability
- Movies and Series validation
- TV validation
- Metadata reliability
- Multi-user behavior
- Navigation and interface consistency
- Performance
- Hardware and browser compatibility
- Real-world testing on clean systems

The objective of the first beta is not to complete every planned Aurora feature.

The objective is to establish a reliable foundation that real users can install, explore and help improve.

## 🏠 Unified Home

Aurora is being designed around a global Home experience connecting its different media worlds.

The main Aurora environment will bring together:

**Music → Movies & Series → TV → News → Karaoke → Guitar**

Each area retains its own dedicated experience while Aurora Home provides a unified view across the platform.

The goal is to make Aurora feel like one product rather than a collection of separate applications.

## 👥 Aurora Sessions

Aurora Sessions is planned as the shared and multi-device layer of the Aurora experience.

The concept includes:

### 🎬 Watch Together

Synchronize movie or series playback between participating Aurora users and devices.

### 🎵 Listen Together

Listen to music together with synchronized playback.

### 🏠 Whole Home

Synchronize playback across multiple compatible devices inside the home.

### 💬 Aurora Chat

Optional communication between Aurora users, designed to integrate naturally with the Aurora experience.

Aurora Chat may eventually allow users to share media, recommendations and Aurora content directly inside conversations.

Aurora Sessions is planned for development after the initial public beta foundation is established.

Aurora Server will continue evolving as the foundation behind every Aurora experience.

- Safe upgrades

- Secure integrations

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Control

Aurora will continue to follow a local-first philosophy.

Core principles include:

- Your media remains under your control
- Local use should not require a mandatory cloud account
- External integrations remain optional
- Sensitive credentials remain server-side
- Aurora should remain useful even without unnecessary cloud dependencies

## 🌅 Beyond the First Release

The first public release is the beginning rather than the end of Aurora.

The objective remains the same:

**Build one coherent personal self-streaming platform for your media, your server and your world.**
