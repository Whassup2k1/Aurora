# 🌌 Aurora Features

Aurora is a personal self-streaming platform designed to bring your media,
entertainment and creative tools together in one unified experience.

**Your media. Your server. Your world.**

## 🔒 Privacy & Local-First

Aurora is built around a local-first philosophy.

- Your personal media remains on infrastructure you control.
- A mandatory cloud account is not required for basic local use.
- External integrations are optional.
- Sensitive integration credentials remain server-side.
- Aurora does not provide commercial media catalogs.

A dedicated privacy document will provide additional details before public
beta distribution.
